# APG

AutoPlan for Golf

> Plan your Japan golf trip in minutes, not days.

## Mission

Reduce 2–3 days of golf trip planning to 5 minutes.

## Vision

APG automatically optimizes flights, golf courses, hotels, and travel routes to recommend the best Japan golf trip based on the user's conditions.

## Philosophy

- User First
- Truth Before Revenue
- Always Free
- Simplicity Wins
